# KSC JARVIS — Brand Guidelines

## Business
- Name: K. Somu Chetty & Co.
- Location: Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.
- Established: 1916.
- Business: wholesale, retail and bulk supply of Indian raw drugs, herbs, pooja and Kumbabhishekam items, wax, sambrani, dhoop sticks and related traditional products.
- Product brands include Indira and Dipak.
- Website: https://ksomuchetty.in/

## Visual identity
- Premium, minimal, elegant and natural.
- Prefer warm cream/off-white backgrounds and restrained gold accents.
- Use clean spacing and clear hierarchy; avoid visual clutter.
- Use the official uploaded company logo exactly as supplied. Do not redraw, stretch, distort, recolour or replace it with generated text.
- Keep the English company name prominent. Tamil company name is only used when specifically requested for a design.

## Product imagery
- Aim for photorealistic product photography with a professional photoshoot feel.
- Show the actual product clearly and accurately.
- Avoid generic leaf decorations when they do not belong to the product.
- Do not invent product claims, certifications, prices or availability.

## Copy and layout
- Keep poster copy concise and product-led.
- Make the product name prominent and readable.
- Do not repeat the company name unnecessarily.
- Prepare square social posts and vertical story/print layouts when requested.

## Avoid
- Dark or black themes.
- Cluttered layouts and excessive copy.
- Modified or inaccurate logos.
- Unrelated celebrity imagery.
- Unsupported health, purity, organic or therapeutic claims.

## Approval and publishing
- Generated artwork is a draft until a person reviews it.
- Do not auto-publish or make product/price claims without explicit approval.
